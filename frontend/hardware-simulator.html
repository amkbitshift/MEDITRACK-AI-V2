How The Hardware Operates Over Wi-Fi (Interactive Functional Steps)

Click any workflow to execute real functional round-trip test
WORKFLOW 1 💳

RFID Asset Identification

When an RFID tag touches the RC522 antenna, the ESP32 reads its 4-byte UID, maps it to the asset, transmits HTTP POST over Wi-Fi, and turns the Green LED solid ON.

WORKFLOW 2 🏃

PIR Motion Auto-Engagement

If PIR detects continuous patient movement on GPIO 32 for 10 seconds, ESP32 sounds the piezo buzzer, sets status to IN_USE, and starts the 30-min usage stopwatch.

WORKFLOW 3 🔘

Pushbutton Session Control

Pressing Switch 1 (GPIO 33) starts or terminates clinical sessions. On END, Red LED pulses for 1s and buzzer beeps twice. Switch 2 (GPIO 13) pauses into Temporary Hold.

WORKFLOW 4 ♿

Clustered Storage Auto-Verify

When WC-007 docks at Radiology Storage, a 120-second countdown runs. If left undisturbed, the backend automatically verifies it back to AVAILABLE status.
